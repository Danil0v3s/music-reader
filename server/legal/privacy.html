Music Reader is a practice app for cellists, run by [operator]. This page says what the Music Reader server keeps about you, why, and how to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and email address if you give them; for Sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier those services give us (with Apple's private relay, we never see your real address); for email sign-in, your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash.
  • Your library: the scores you create, import or scan, the original PDFs and photos of imported scores, and your collections. They're yours: nobody else sees them unless you share a code for them.
  • Your device: a random identifier the app makes on first launch (we store only a one-way hash of it), the app version, and which accounts have used it. We use it to keep free scans to a fair number per device and to stop abuse. It isn't used for advertising or tracking.
  • Your scans: for each recognition, when it ran, the file name and type, the number of pages, and whether it worked. The uploaded file itself is deleted within a day.
  • Sessions: sign-in tokens (stored as hashes) and when they were last used.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics. Account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password) are sent through an email provider.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). That removes your account, scores, originals, collections and sessions straight away, and revokes Sign in with Apple. The scan log keeps its entries without your account, attached only to the device's hashed identifier, so free scans can't be reset by deleting and recreating accounts. Guest accounts unused for a year are deleted automatically. Backups roll over within [14] days.
